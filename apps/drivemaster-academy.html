Frequently Asked Questions

What is DriveMaster Academy?

DriveMaster Academy is a digital learning platform designed to help learners study driving theory, road signs and road safety.

Is it a replacement for driving school?

No. It is an educational support tool. Practical driving instruction should be obtained from a qualified instructor where required.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. The website is responsive and designed for phones, tablets and desktop computers.

Will there be practice tests?

Yes. Practice questions and quizzes are included in the learning experience.

Where can I get the app?

Use the Launch Web App or Google Play buttons when the corresponding version is available.
